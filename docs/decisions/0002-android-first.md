# 0002: Android first
Status: Accepted · 2026-10-18

**Context.** iOS does not let third-party apps read or draw over other apps' screens. In India, Android has the large majority of phones: vivo 21%, Samsung 15%, Oppo 13% and Xiaomi 13% of 2025 shipments ([Omdia via GSMArena](https://m.gsmarena.com/vivo_led_indias_smartphone_market_in_2025_with_doubledigit_shipment_growth-amp-71181.php)).

**Decision.** Build for Android first. iOS may get a static guide mode later.

**Consequences.** We have to handle several brand skins. US iPhone users are left out for now.
