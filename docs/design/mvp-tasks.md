# MVP: the first 10 tasks (draft for review)

These were chosen to mix phone settings (which change with the phone brand) with popular apps (which change weekly), so that both hard parts are tested early.

| # | Task (EN / HI) | App | Why it's in | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Make screen brighter / Brightness बढ़ाइए | Settings | The classic example; differs by brand | Easy |
| 2 | Make text bigger / font बड़ा करिए | Settings | Huge for elders; differs by brand | Easy |
| 3 | Connect Bluetooth earphones or speaker / Bluetooth connect करिए | Settings | Multi-step, involves pairing pop-ups | Medium |
| 4 | Connect to Wi-Fi / Wi-Fi connect करिए | Settings | Involves typing a password, which we never read: guide only | Medium |
| 5 | Search and play a video (e.g. yoga) / video search करिए | YouTube | Most-used app; tests search plus results | Medium |
| 6 | Video call someone / video call करिए | WhatsApp | Top reason elders get smartphones | Medium |
| 7 | Send a photo / photo भेजिए | WhatsApp | Gallery picker plus chat | Medium |
| 8 | Play a song / गाना चलाइए | Spotify or JioSaavn | Mridul's example; JioSaavn may be more common in India (unverified) | Medium |
| 9 | Google something by voice / बोलकर search करिए | Google app | Gateway to independence | Easy |
| 10 | Find a dish and add to cart, **stop before payment** / खाना ढूँढिए | Zomato or Swiggy | Tests a fast-changing app; checks the "no payment" rule | Hard |

**Test phones (one each, ideally from family):** Samsung (One UI), vivo (Funtouch/OriginOS), Oppo or Realme (ColorOS), Xiaomi or Redmi (HyperOS), plus a stock Pixel emulator.

**Success criteria for the MVP**
- A first-time older user finishes 8 of 10 tasks **without human help** on their own phone.
- Recipes still work after one month of app updates on at least 3 of the 4 brands. This measures how much upkeep there is.
